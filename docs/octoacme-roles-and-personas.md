# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure quality standards are met and acceptance criteria are validated. They own the testing strategy, coordinate test automation, and triage quality issues.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans and QA approach
- Create and maintain automated test suites
- Perform manual testing for complex scenarios
- Triage and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Partner with developers on test coverage and CI automation
- Validate acceptance criteria before release

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases
- Reduce post-release defects
- Maintain fast feedback loops through automated testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement discussions
- Test status reports and defect logs
- Release readiness sign-off

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system solutions, define technical standards, and ensure scalability, security, and maintainability. They guide technical decisions across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and system architecture
- Define coding standards, patterns, and best practices
- Review designs and technical implementations
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Mentor developers on technical excellence
- Advise on technology choices and dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Foster consistent, high-quality engineering practices

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and RFCs
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review feedback and technical mentoring

---

## Security Officer

### Role Summary
Security Officers embed security into project planning and execution. They assess risks, ensure compliance, and guide secure development practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security impact and compliance requirements
- Participate in design reviews and threat modeling
- Coordinate security scanning and penetration testing
- Advise on secure coding practices and authentication/authorization patterns
- Escalate security incidents and coordinate response
- Maintain security checklists and audit logs

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant solutions
- Minimize security vulnerabilities in production
- Foster security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat assessments
- Security incident notifications and post-mortems
- Compliance and audit reporting

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate iterative delivery, remove process blockers, and help teams improve their ways of working.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and escalate impediments
- Coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint health and velocity metrics
- Facilitate communication and psychological safety
- Adapt processes based on team feedback

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and flow
- Foster continuous improvement and learning culture
- Enable teams to self-organize and deliver predictably

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective action items and improvements
- Metrics dashboards and sprint health reports

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and resources. They champion the project and represent key interests (business, leadership, customers).

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and success metrics
- Approve scope, budget, and timeline decisions
- Escalate blockers and remove organizational barriers
- Communicate project status and outcomes to their organization
- Provide feedback on deliverables and user impact

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers intended business value
- Maintain alignment and buy-in across the organization
- Support team with resources and organizational support

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and planning gate reviews
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Decision gates and approval sign-offs
- Incident and escalation notifications

---

## Support / Customer Success Lead

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success teams are the voice of the customer during delivery. They ensure solutions are operationally supported and deliver real customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Provide customer feedback and use-case validation during planning
- Partner with QA and developers on usability and operability
- Prepare support materials, FAQs, and runbooks
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Monitor early adoption and gather customer feedback post-release
- Identify support gaps and escalate critical issues

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are operationally supportable
- Maximize customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce support burden and escalations

### Typical Communication
- Planning and requirements gathering sessions
- User acceptance testing (UAT) participation
- Release communication and training
- Post-release monitoring and customer feedback loops
